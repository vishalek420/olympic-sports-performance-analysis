[0:45 - 2:05] PART 2: SCENARIO 1 — MS. PRIYA (NOC PERFORMANCE STRATEGIST)
(Tone: Strategic, analytical, executive)

"Sabse pehle milte hain hamari pehli stakeholder se — Ms. Priya, jo ek National Olympic Committee mein Performance Strategist hain.

Priya ka challenge hai upcoming Olympic cycle ke liye national funding allocate karna: kaun se sports categories mein invest karein jahan hamare athletes sabse zyada competitive hon, aur kin disciplines se funding withdraw karein?

Priya refer karti hain hamare 'Avg Medals per Athlete by Sport Category' horizontal bar chart ko. Yahan data ek shocking revelation deta hai:

Rowing 6.916 medals per athlete ke saath pure Olympic spectrum mein highest medal yield generate karta hai! Iske theek peeche Shooting hai at 6.731 medals per athlete, followed by Swimming at 5.82 aur Canoeing at 5.41. 

Iska matlab kya hai? Single athlete multiple boat classes ya multiple shooting events mein participate karke country ke liye multiple medals la sakta hai. Dusri taraf, Football at 0.69 ya Basketball at 0.82 jaise large team sports mein 20 se 25 athletes bhejne ke baad bhi maximum ek medal milta hai.

Priya is insight ko cross-reference karti hain hamare Global Performance Metrics chart ke saath, jahan United States 735 medals aur China 696 medals ke saath global benchmarks hain. 

Priya ka recommendation clear hai: middle-tier nations ko apne limited resources Rowing aur Shooting jaise high-yield categories mein concentrate karne chahiye taaki future Games mein unka overall medal haul maximize ho sake." [pause 1.5s]

[2:05 - 3:35] PART 3: SCENARIO 2 — MR. CARLOS (SPORTS INVESTMENT ANALYST)
(Tone: Inquisitive, data-backed, consulting)

"Ab aate hain hamare doosre persona par — Mr. Carlos, senior analyst at an international sports funding agency.

Carlos ka mission hai funding grants allocate karna aur yeh analyze karna ki kis country ka financial investment actual Olympic medals mein sabse efficiently convert hota hai.

Carlos use karte hain hamara 'Global Sports Investment vs. Medal Performance' Bubble Chart. Yahan X-axis par Millions of Dollars invested hain aur Y-axis par total medal count hai, jabki bubble size Medal Efficiency Percentage ko indicate karta hai.

Aur yahan ek fascinating paradox nikal kar aata hai! USA aur China ka spend massive hai — 800 to 900 Million dollars. Lekin efficiency ke terms mein असली overperformers hain:
Slovakia — jisne sirf $22M spend karke 38 medals jeete at 59.5% efficiency.
Georgia — $21M ke investment par 42 medals aur 61.9% efficiency.
Aur Ethiopia — jisne sirf $26M ke budget par 58 Olympic medals clinch kiye at a staggering 64.8% efficiency!

Ab aate hain Carlos ke Regional Efficiency Donut Chart par:
Data proves that South America leads global regional efficiency at 49.64%, followed closely by Europe/Asia at 45.98%. 

Yeh data Carlos ke liye game-changer hai. Iske basis par international agencies rich nations ko subsidies dene ke bajaye Latin America aur African nations ke grassroots sports programs ko targeted grants distribute kar sakti hain, jahan har ek dollar ka return 3 times zyada podium finishes deliver karta hai." [pause 1.5s]
